Questions

How much does it cost?

The app is free. Extra features come with the BoBudget Pro subscription.

Where is my data stored?

On a secured server in Europe. Personal fields are encrypted and every connection uses HTTPS. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Account → Delete account. Or without the app, on the account deletion page.

When will it be in the stores?

Testing is under way. BoBudget already works in Telegram — you can try it there.

Is this financial advice?

No. BoBudget is a tracking tool; the decisions are yours.
